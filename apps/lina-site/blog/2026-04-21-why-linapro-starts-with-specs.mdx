AI can generate code quickly. That is not the same thing as shipping a durable product quickly.

LinaPro starts with specs because the real cost in AI-assisted projects usually shows up later:

{/* truncate */}

- teams repeat context in every session
- acceptance criteria live in chat instead of tests
- architecture drifts one small contradiction at a time
- human review becomes the slowest part of the system

OpenSpec gives LinaPro a way to slow down the right things and speed up the rest.

The loop is simple:

```text
Explore -> Propose -> Implement -> Review -> Archive
```

What matters is that every phase leaves behind artifacts that both humans and AI can use in the next iteration.

That is why LinaPro does not frame specs as bureaucracy. It treats them as operational memory.
